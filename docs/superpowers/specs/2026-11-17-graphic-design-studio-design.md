# Graphic Design Studio — design

Date: 17 Nov 2026 · Status: draft v2 for approval (owner's answers of 17 Nov folded in)

## 1. Intent

DM Group has one in-house designer, and design work is scattered across files and tools:

- Logos and brand files live on individual machines.
- Business cards are made in a separate Base44 app (dmdocument), and the PDF link is typed into the ERP by hand.
- Email signatures are inconsistent.
- Design requests arrive informally.

The Studio brings all of this into the ERP:

- **The designer** keeps one library of approved brand assets. They build templates by uploading a background and placing fields on it, and choose which brands each employee may use.
- **The ERP generates designs from employee records:**
  - email signatures, which every employee can get for themselves;
  - business cards, generated and printed only by the IT staff the designer selects;
  - recognition certificates, generated only by the HR staff the designer selects.
- **Every employee** can request a design and follow it in "My requests". They get an email when it's delivered.

**Scope is design only.** Official HR documents (salary, experience or no-objection letters, contracts) stay in `DM Letter Forms` and are out of scope.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**
1. Today's DM Group, Qimsco and Najd cards are reproduced with the same look (parity test, §8). DMCC, Asas and FCrystal have no working design today; they get one when the designer uploads it.
2. Templates are made by uploading a background (PDF/PNG/JPG) and placing fields visually. No HTML or code.
3. Print output is vector: the original background PDF is kept and real text with embedded fonts is placed on it, with optional 3 mm bleed and crop marks. Screen output is PNG.
4. Arabic text is shaped correctly, and every Arabic value comes from a stored, human-approved field, never a fresh machine translation.
5. Access matches the owner's rules (§6):
   - one designer manages everything;
   - certificates are visible to and generated by selected HR staff only;
   - business cards are visible to and generated by selected IT staff only;
   - every employee gets their signature, the request form and "My requests".
6. A delivered design request emails the requester, and the result is visible in "My requests".
7. Bulk generation (a department, a brand, or all active employees) runs in the background without slowing the ERP.
8. Every generated file records the template version and the data snapshot that produced it.

**Non-goals**
- Official/legal HR documents.
- A drawing tool. Artwork is made in the designer's own software.
- Social-media scheduling or analytics. The empty custom `Marketing *` doctypes are left untouched.
- Employees downloading their own business card or certificates (the owner wants these limited to IT and HR).

**Anti-goals (failure even if it works)**
- A card that differs from the approved design, or blurry printed text.
- An employee's Arabic name spelled differently from one output to the next.
- Anyone outside the selected HR/IT staff seeing certificates or cards.
- A request marked delivered without the requester being told.
- Large source files (PSD/AI/video) bloating ERP backups.
- Browsing, requests and existing signatures breaking when the ceopanel is down. Only *new* generation may depend on it.

## 4. Approaches considered

| | Approach | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| A | ERP only (doctypes + Print Formats + block) | No new infrastructure, but the ERP's PDF renderer handles CSS and Arabic poorly. No PNG output, no previews of uploaded PDFs, and bulk runs block ERP workers. |
| **B** | **ERP for records and UI; ceopanel as the render engine** | Reuses the PDF Editor's proven relay and worker path: vector PDF with correct Arabic, PNG, previews and background jobs. Only new generation depends on the ceopanel. |
| C | Everything in the ceopanel | A second login, and the ceopanel's business dashboards were deliberately retired. |

**Chosen: B** (owner, 17 Nov). Within B:
- Cards and certificates: PyMuPDF writes shaped text onto the original background page (vector).
- Signatures: Chromium renders HTML to PNG, plus email-safe HTML.

| **Recognition Certificate** | HR's certificate record | template, employees (child), award_title, reason, award_date, issued_by, status (Draft / Issued / Revoked), verify_code |
| **Design Studio Settings** (Single) | Access the designer controls | designer (User), certificate_issuers (child: User), card_operators (child: User), request notification options |

**Employee additions** (custom fields, owner approval needed before creating):
- `custom_name_ar` + `custom_name_ar_verified`.
- `custom_design_brands`: a Table MultiSelect of the brands the designer has allowed this employee, defaulting to DM Group.
- `custom_signature_brand`: the employee's own choice among the allowed brands.

**Designation addition:** `custom_designation_ar` + verified flag, entered once per designation and reused by everyone with it.

**Missing Arabic values:**
- The Studio suggests a translation and a person accepts or edits it; nothing is stored unconfirmed.
- Templates that use an unverified Arabic field are blocked for that employee, with the reason shown.

**`Business Card` (existing):** kept as the record of printed cards. IT generates from it, and `dm_card_pdf_link` is filled automatically with the Design Output file. Its company select maps 1:1 to Design Brand.

### 5.3 Template editor

- Upload the front (and optional back) background.
  - PDF is preferred and keeps vector content.
  - PNG/JPG must be ≥ 300 dpi at print size, with a warning below that.
  - Page size comes from the PDF; presets cover 90 × 50 mm, 85 × 55 mm, A4/A5 and the signature banner.
- The canvas shows the engine's preview of the background at true aspect.
  - Drag and resize field boxes, with snap and alignment guides.
  - Arrow keys nudge 0.5 mm, Shift+arrow 5 mm.
  - Bleed and safe-area overlays.
- Field sources:
  - **Employee fields:** name EN/AR, designation EN/AR, department, mobile, company email, photo, joining date, years of service.
  - **Brand fields:** website, address EN/AR, VIDs.
  - **Computed:** vCard QR, link QR, verify QR, date.
  - **Asked at generation:** award title, reason.
  - **Static text.**
- Styling: brand-kit fonts only, any colour (kit colours offered first), alignment, direction, shrink-to-fit floor.
- **Live preview with a real employee:** a picker steps through employees, and overflow, wrapping or missing data is highlighted.
- One designer, so there's no second approval. **Publish** creates a new version, retires the previous one, and marks outputs from the old version as stale.

### 5.4 Engine — ceopanel module `design-studio`

Built on the PDF Editor pattern:
- `app/api/design-studio/v1/relay/route.ts`, behind the ERP API Server Script `durar_design_studio.py`. The script forwards the session user, the permission-checked data snapshot and the shared relay secret.
- BullMQ jobs on the existing worker.
- Python processor `lib/design-studio/processor.py`.
- Additive migration `0xx-design-studio.sql` (job state only; ERP stays the source of truth).

Jobs:
- `preview`: background or asset → PNG preview and thumbnail (PDF, SVG, PNG/JPG, AI-with-PDF; PSD only if it contains a composite).
- `render-card` / `render-certificate`: open the background page, then:
  - write fields with PyMuPDF (HarfBuzz shaping, embedded font subsets);
  - shrink to fit down to the floor, otherwise fail with a named reason;
  - add photo, QR, and bleed/crop marks;
  - output PDF + 300 dpi PNG.
- `render-signature`: HTML → PNG with Chromium, plus email-safe HTML (tables, inline styles, absolute image URL).
- `render-batch`: fan out the renders, impose cards on A4 with crop marks, zip the PNGs.

Results are written back as ERP File attachments on Design Output. Signature PNGs are saved as *public* ERP files with random names so mail clients can load them; everything else stays private.

### 5.6 Extras (builder's proposals; easy to drop)

- **Stale detection:**
  - when a designation, mobile or email changes after a signature or card was generated, that output is marked stale;
  - the employee sees "Your signature is out of date — refresh";
  - IT sees stale cards.
- **Signature campaigns:** the designer schedules a banner under all signatures of chosen brands (Eid, Ramadan, National Day, exhibitions) with start and end dates. Signatures switch automatically.
- **Personal vCard QR:** the DM Group card back has one static QR today. The template can swap it for a per-employee vCard QR, so scanning saves that person's contact. It's off by default to match today's card.
- **Print order sheet:** a card batch also exports a CSV (name, brand, quantity) for the print shop.
- **Request turnaround:** average days from Submitted to Delivered, shown on Usage.

## 6. Permissions

| Who | How identified | Can |
|---|---|---|
| Designer (1 person) | `Design Studio Settings.designer` + new role **Design Manager** | Everything in the Studio |
| Certificate issuers (HR, selected by designer) | `certificate_issuers` table | See the Certificates tab; issue and revoke certificates |
| Card operators (IT, selected by designer) | `card_operators` table | See the Business cards tab; generate, print and record cards for any employee |
| Every employee with an ERP user | Employee.user_id | View brand kits and published library; get their own signature; submit requests and see only their own |

Server Scripts check these lists on every call, and the block only hides what the server would refuse anyway. Requests are filtered to `requested_by = session user` for everyone except the designer.

## 7. Migration from dmdocument (Base44)

1. Download the backgrounds from the Base44 media URLs in the export: the DM Group PDF, the Qimsco PNGs and the Najd PNGs. Store them as Design Assets of type Background.
2. **Check before migrating:** the Qimsco back background is named `DMR_BC_MohammadNuzair.png`. Confirm it's a blank master and not one employee's finished card.
3. Convert the fields:
   - pt → mm;
   - bottom-left → top-left origin, using the page height (168.83 mm for DM Group/Qimsco, 312 pt for Najd);
   - right-anchored Arabic boxes become right-aligned boxes;
   - fonts: Montserrat 700 / Tinos 700 / Tajawal 700, as rendered today.

   If the designer wants the intended GE MB / Garet fonts, they upload the licensed files to the brand kit and switch.
4. **Parity test:** render 10 real employees per migrated brand in both systems and compare. Positions must match within 0.5 mm.
5. Repoint the 3 `Business Card` links to the new outputs. Run both systems for one print cycle, then retire dmdocument.

## 8. Phases (each deployed only with the owner's approval)

1. **Foundation:**
   - module, DocTypes, role and settings;
   - Brand kits and Library tabs;
   - engine skeleton (relay + preview job);
   - Employees tab with brand assignment and the Arabic-name review queue.
2. **Requests:** Employee Dashboard form and My requests, designer board, delivery upload and email notifications. Doesn't depend on the engine, so it can ship first if wanted.
3. **Templates and signatures:**
   - template editor and signature rendering;
   - Employee Dashboard "My email signature";
   - `User.email_signature` sync.
4. **Business cards:** migration and parity test, IT generation and print batches, `Business Card` integration.
5. **Certificates:** HR issuing and the verify page. Then Usage, campaigns and stale detection.

## 9. Decided by the owner

- Approach B. Design only, no official documents.
- Templates are made by uploading a background and mapping text onto it.
- The current card designs must be reproduced and stay in use.
- One designer.
- Employees get their assigned email signature, choosing among brands the designer allows them.
- Certificates are generated only by HR staff the designer selects, and only they can see them.
- Business cards are generated and printed only by IT staff the designer selects, and only they can see them.
- Every employee gets a request form and a "My requests" area. The designer sees requests and uploads finished designs, and the requester gets an email when it's done.
- The builder may add features as they see fit.

## 10. Builder's calls (open to correction)

- **Brands** are their own list (Design Brand), not ERP companies, because all active employees are on one ERP company.
- **Signature images** are public ERP files with random names; everything else is private.
- **Certificate verification** uses a guest Server Script that returns only name, award, date, brand and status for a verify code, and is rate-limited.
- **Card size** comes from the background PDF; presets apply only to image backgrounds.
- **No approval step** for templates, since there is one designer.
- **Requests** can ship before the engine (phase 2), because they need only ERP doctypes, the dashboards and email.
- **Fonts:** Montserrat, Poppins, Tinos and Tajawal are open-licence and embeddable. Commercial fonts need their licence file in the brand kit.

## 11. Left to the builder

- Imposition layout (cards per A4) and PNG dpi.
- KPI thresholds and attention rules.
- Thumbnail fallback for formats without a composite.
- Job time limits and retries (follow the PDF Editor's limits).
- Email wording (EN/AR) and the exact Employee Dashboard placement after reading that block's current layout.
